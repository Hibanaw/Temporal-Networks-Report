\subsection{Cross-network summary}\label{sec:r-cross}
\Cref{tab:cross} lists one measure per hypothesis. Because the measures differ in units
and interpretation, the table does not define a single sensitivity score for a network type:
H1 is descriptive, H2--H6 are differences between the real value and the null mean in the
units of the respective metric, and H7 is a conditional ratio of peak times.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering\small
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}llrrr@{}}

H2 & $\Delta B$ vs P1 & 0.016 & 0.336 & -- \\
H3 & $\Delta J$ vs P1 & $-0.147$ & 0.027 & -- \\
H4 & $\Delta\alpha$ vs P3a & -- & -- & 0.713 \\

\bottomrule\end{tabular*}
\caption{Hypothesis-specific measures. H1 uses different grids and definitions (PS 5\,min--24\,h,

\label{tab:cross}
\end{table*}

In summary, H1 holds in all three networks; H2 holds for burstiness, with a small and
interval-dependent PS excess; the CM H3 contrast is positive over the full period but
negative in both halves; H4 holds except for diameter shrinkage and forecast advantage; H5
holds in PS but not in CM; and H6 shows lower reachability than both nulls under the
finite-horizon protocol. H7 is conditionally supported in PS and not supported in CM, and
neither the choice of reference model nor the conditioning on major outbreaks can be omitted
from this statement.
