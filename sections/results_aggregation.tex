\subsection{Representation and time windows (H1)}
\label{sec:r-aggregation}
\paragraph{Contact and messaging networks.}
Every structural metric changes substantially with the window width in both networks
(\cref{tab:a-windows}, \cref{fig:agg-window}a,b). Wider windows combine interactions that
occurred at different times and therefore increase density, mean degree, clustering and
connectivity while reducing the number of components. In PS the mean LCC fraction rises
from 11.6\% in 5-min windows to 56.3\% in 8-h windows and reaches 99.6\% in the single
complete 24-h window. In CM it rises from 0.24\% at 1\,h to 35.8\% at 30\,days (six complete
windows). The number of components in CM varies least (sensitivity 0.398), because most
users remain isolated in all but the widest windows.

\paragraph{Citation network.}
In 2020 the annual and cumulative graphs on the same 19,150 published papers contain 1,450
and 73,269 citations, with mean total degree 0.151 and 7.652 and LCC fraction 0.052 and
0.641, respectively. Cumulative clustering is 0.124, whereas annual clustering is
identically zero: because citations point to strictly older papers, every annual snapshot
is bipartite. This is a property of the representation and not evidence of temporal
correlation. Over trailing windows of 1--10 years, the sensitivities are 1.798 for density,
2.361 for clustering and 1.114 for the LCC fraction (\cref{fig:agg-window}c). Longer
windows connect a larger fraction of papers and admit triangles spanning several years,
which a single final aggregate cannot reveal.

\paragraph{Observation halves.}
The same directions hold at every comparable width in both halves of both networks. On
the common grid, the PS sensitivities are stable (density 2.503 over the full period
against 2.551 and 2.518 in the halves; clustering 1.394 against 1.470 and 1.487). The
magnitude of connectivity in CM, however, depends on the period: at 30\,days the mean LCC
fraction is 53.3\% in the first half and 18.6\% in the second (three complete windows each),
which coincides with a decline in activity from 53,163 to 6,635 messages.

\paragraph{Verdict on H1.}
H1 is supported in all three networks as a representation effect. The numerical
sensitivity depends on the window grid, the window alignment, the inclusion of inactive
nodes and the observation span, so the values in \cref{tab:a-windows} do not rank the
network types. Connectivity after aggregation also discards event order; whether
aggregated paths can be traversed in time is examined under H6.
